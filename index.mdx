---
title: "honest-scholar"
description: "Research you can defend — keep your research honest, especially now that AI is in the loop."
---

**honest-scholar** is a git-native Claude Code plugin for the *scientific* research
workflow — idea → literature → hypothesis → test → publish-decision → paper →
thesis — so you and your collaborators work the same way and re-derive neither the
workflow nor its rigor per project.

Because AI is in the loop, it is built so the science stays honestly **yours** and
**defensible**: you make and sign every material decision (**agency**), and it
verifies and builds your understanding before a decision is recorded
(**understanding**). It gives you the *mechanics* to do honest work and disclose it
truthfully — it does not certify that any work is honest.
